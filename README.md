# Greenwood Navigator

Give me as much as you can in free credits, Create a highly experimental, state-of-the-art interactive prototype that transforms the supplied Lift Greenwood staff hierarchy image into an explorable digital experience.

THIS IS NOT A NORMAL ORGANISATION CHART.

THIS IS NOT A STANDARD WEBSITE OR DASHBOARD.

I want the exact hierarchy from the supplied image to become an interactive visual map that users can physically explore.

The client should look at this and think:

“How did you even turn a simple hierarchy into this?”

SOURCE OF TRUTH

The uploaded hierarchy image is the ONLY source of truth.

Preserve exactly:

- every person

- every name

- every photograph

- every job title

- every relationship

- every level

- every reporting connection

Do not invent people or information.

CORE IDEA:

Imagine the hierarchy as a living digital map.

Instead of reading boxes from top to bottom, users can:

- drag the hierarchy around

- zoom in and out

- pan across it

- click individual people

- follow relationships

- reveal connections

- move between leadership levels

- isolate teams

- explore the four Houses

- zoom from the entire organisation into an individual person

The interaction should feel closer to exploring an interactive map than looking at an org chart.

==================================================

THE HERO EXPERIENCE

==================================================

Start with an almost empty dark/clean canvas.

At the centre is the highest-level person from the hierarchy.

The person card appears.

A small line begins drawing outward/downward.

The next level appears.

Then the next.

Then the next.

The complete hierarchy gradually builds itself.

Once loaded, DO NOT leave the hierarchy static.

The entire hierarchy should be draggable.

The user can grab empty space and move around the organisation.

The user can use:

- mouse wheel to zoom

- trackpad pinch to zoom

- click and drag to pan

- buttons for zoom in/out

- reset view

Make the movement smooth and premium.

==================================================

THE “PLAY WITH THE HIERARCHY” EXPERIENCE

==================================================

This is the most important part.

When the user hovers over a person:

Their card subtly enlarges.

A soft visual pulse travels along their reporting connection.

Their immediate connections become brighter/more prominent.

Unrelated people become slightly muted.

This should visually answer:

“Who does this person connect to?”

When the user clicks a person:

DO NOT simply open a boring popup.

Instead:

1. Lock the selected person into focus.

2. Smoothly zoom the camera towards them.

3. Highlight their manager above.

4. Highlight their direct reports below.

5. Animate the connecting lines.

6. Slightly dim everyone else.

7. Show a small elegant information panel.

The user should feel like they have entered that person's part of the organisation.

==================================================

FOLLOW THE CONNECTION

==================================================

Create a “Follow connection” interaction.

When a person is selected, show a small control:

“Explore connection →”

Clicking it should animate the camera along the hierarchy.

For example:

Person A

↓

Manager

↓

Senior leadership

↓

House leadership

The camera smoothly travels through the structure.

The user is effectively navigating the hierarchy like a visual journey.

==================================================

THE FOUR HOUSE CONNECTION

==================================================

The four Heads of House should have a particularly impressive interaction.

When the user reaches the House level, visually separate the four House leaders while maintaining their connection to the main organisation.

Create four distinct visual nodes.

Use their existing House colours if available.

When hovering over one House:

That House expands slightly.

Its connecting line becomes active.

The other Houses become slightly quieter.

Clicking a House should create a beautiful zoom transition into that House.

Show its Head and relevant connection from the hierarchy.

The four Houses should visually feel like:

FOUR DISTINCT COMMUNITIES

BUT

ONE GREENWOOD

==================================================

ZOOM LEVELS

==================================================

Create different levels of information depending on zoom.

ZOOMED OUT:

The user sees the entire Greenwood structure.

Cards become simplified.

Only photos/names are prominent.

ZOOMED IN:

Cards reveal job titles.

Connections become clearer.

ZOOMED VERY CLOSE:

Individual person cards become detailed.

This makes the hierarchy feel like a genuine interactive information map.

==================================================

MAGNETIC / ORGANIC MOVEMENT

==================================================

Do NOT make every card feel rigidly locked into a boring rectangular grid.

Keep the hierarchy accurate, but introduce subtle organic movement.

Cards should have very subtle floating/parallax movement.

Connector lines should intelligently follow the nodes.

When the user drags a section, connected elements should move naturally with it.

Do not allow the hierarchy to become chaotic or lose its reporting structure.

Accuracy of relationships is more important than visual experimentation.

==================================================

“CONSTELLATION” IDEA

==================================================

Explore a visual language inspired by a constellation/map.

People are the points.

Relationships are the connecting lines.

Leadership sits at the centre/top.

The Houses form important connected clusters.

When a person is selected, their network lights up.

But DO NOT make this look like a sci-fi interface.

It must remain appropriate for a premium UK school.

Sophisticated.

Editorial.

Human.

Modern.

==================================================

HIDDEN INTERACTION

==================================================

Add a subtle “Explore” indicator.

For example:

“Drag to explore”

“Scroll to zoom”

“Select a person”

After the user begins interacting, these instructions disappear.

Do not overload the screen with controls.

==================================================

MINI MAP

==================================================

When zoomed in, show a tiny minimap in one corner.

It should show where the user currently is within the full hierarchy.

The user can click the minimap to jump to another area.

This should feel like exploring a large digital map.

==================================================

RESET / OVERVIEW

==================================================

Add a discreet control:

“View whole organisation”

When clicked:

The camera smoothly zooms back out.

All muted people return to normal.

The entire hierarchy becomes visible again.

==================================================

VISUAL STYLE

==================================================

Premium UK school.

Not corporate.

Not SaaS.

Not a dashboard.

Not a generic org-chart template.

Not AI-looking.

Use:

- sophisticated typography

- large amounts of breathing space

- restrained school brand colours

- subtle shadows

- refined borders

- high-quality image treatment

- elegant motion

- smooth camera movement

- very subtle depth

- excellent responsive behaviour

The photography should remain authentic.

Do not generate replacement portraits.

Do not use stock photography.

==================================================

THE WOW MOMENT

==================================================

The most important demo moment:

Start at the full organisation.

Click one person.

The camera smoothly travels into that person.

Their manager above becomes visible.

Their direct reports appear.

Then select one of those people.

The camera follows the connection.

Continue down until reaching the House structure.

Then zoom back out.

The entire organisation returns into view.

This should make the hierarchy feel like a connected living system rather than a static picture.

==================================================

IMPORTANT TECHNICAL CONSTRAINT

==================================================

This is a visual prototype.

Keep the implementation lightweight and achievable.

Do not build a backend.

Do not create unnecessary authentication.

Do not add unnecessary complex functionality.

Use lightweight front-end techniques and CSS/JavaScript animations.

Prioritise:

60fps-feeling interactions

smooth transforms

fast loading

responsive design

working mouse interactions

working touch interactions

working zoom/pan

working selection states

All interactions shown in the prototype must actually work.

==================================================

MOBILE

==================================================

On mobile, do not simply shrink the desktop version.

Create a touch-first exploration experience.

Users should be able to:

- drag

- pinch zoom

- tap people

- follow connections

- return to overview

Keep the hierarchy readable.

==================================================

FINAL CREATIVE PRINCIPLE

==================================================

DO NOT DESIGN AN ORG CHART.

DESIGN AN EXPERIENCE FOR EXPLORING AN ORG CHART.

The existing hierarchy is the raw material.

The prototype should demonstrate what happens when that static image becomes:

INTERACTIVE

EXPLORABLE

ANIMATED

CONNECTED

RESPONSIVE

CLICKABLE

The client should be able to play with it for 30 seconds and immediately understand the value.

Make it feel like a genuinely custom piece of digital design that a high-end creative agency could present as an original concept.

The exact hierarchy from the uploaded image must remain factually accurate.
